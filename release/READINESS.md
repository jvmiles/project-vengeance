# Source-only beta readiness

**GO for the source-only v0.1.0-beta.1 beta; publication authorization pending.**

The frozen-source clean build passed with no missing runtime libraries. On that
exact binary, explicit GDraw passed final PNG options opening/cancellation, JPEG
preview/control usability and normal shutdown. The five GUI observations are
user-attested; binary/archive hashes, exit zero and absence of GTK criticals or
assertion/crash markers are machine-verified. One ordinary theme warning remains.

Earlier native modal lifetime and JPEG decode tests passed. Earlier user reports
confirm XAR fixtures/edit/save/reopen, JPEG and raster-PDF export, with incomplete
operation-specific renderer attribution. Prior integrated AI/MCP/Ollama acceptance
belongs to its recorded binary, not a full retest of the final compiled binary.

Cairo final PNG/JPEG parity and detailed clipboard/text/group/transforms/renderer
comparisons remain NOT TESTED where no specific evidence exists. SVG and native
vector PDF are unsupported; Cairo/Ollama are experimental. No portable binary
package or byte-reproducibility claim. See RELEASE_NOTES.md and THIRD_PARTY.md.

The public package retains complete compiled derivative source and unmodified
CDraw archives/notices. All non-README/release files match the frozen distribution.
Private history, recovery bundles, checkpoints, raw logs and operational reports
are excluded. Snapshot audit is heuristic, not universal secret/legal certification.
